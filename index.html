Cancellation & refunds

You may cancel after payment, any time before purchasing begins, for a full refund. The cancellation cutoff is when an item is actually purchased, not when an agent is assigned or starts travelling.

Once purchasing begins, items already purchased cannot be cancelled or refunded. Items not yet purchased remain eligible for cancellation and refund. After all items have been purchased, the order cannot be cancelled.

Refunds are handled manually to the bank account the original transfer came from. We do not offer wallet credits or promise instant processing. Your progress corner will show refund updates.

If an item is unavailable, we may suggest a substitute. You’ll have five minutes after the request is made available to accept or decline. If you decline or don’t respond in time, that item is removed and its cost is due to be refunded manually.

Stay in the loop

Allow notifications to hear when your request is approved, a substitute is suggested, or your food is on the way. We’ll ask your browser for permission only after you choose to turn them on.

Add a WhatsApp number on your request if you’d like an agent to message you manually.

Add Pass the Plate to your Home Screen

To install on iPhone or iPad, open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then choose Add to Home Screen and tap Add.

Open Pass the Plate from its new Home Screen icon. To receive order alerts, allow notifications from the installed app. Web Push requires iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later.
